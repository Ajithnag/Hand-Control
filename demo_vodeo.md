# Short Demo Video (60–75s)

Use **Windows Game Bar** to record:
- Start/stop recording: `Win + Alt + R`

Goal: show the *minimum* a new user needs to succeed.

---

## One-take script (what to do + what to say)

### 0:00–0:05 — Launch
- **Do**: Open the project folder and double-click `START.bat`.
- **Say**: “Double-click `START.bat` to start. First run may install dependencies.”

### 0:05–0:15 — Confirm it’s ready
- **Do**: Show the camera window HUD.
- **Say**: “Make sure it says **Mode: CURSOR** in green. If it’s **MEDIA** in red, press **m**.”
- **Do**: Put your hand in view until you see hand landmarks / detection.
- **Say**: “When your hand is detected, you’ll see the fingertip marker.”

### 0:15–0:27 — Move the mouse
- **Do**: Move your index finger; show cursor moving.
- **Say**: “Move your **index fingertip** to move the cursor.”

### 0:27–0:38 — Fix speed + calibration (the two big ‘it feels wrong’ fixes)
- **Do**: Press `]` a few times.
- **Say**: “If it’s slow, press **]** to speed up.”
- **Do**: Center your hand and press `c`.
- **Say**: “If it feels constrained, press **c** to calibrate.”

### 0:38–0:52 — Click + double click
- **Do**: Hover an icon/button. Do a quick **tap** gesture (down-up).
- **Say**: “Quick tap = left click.”
- **Do**: Do two quick taps.
- **Say**: “Two quick taps = double click.”

### 0:52–1:05 — Drag
- **Do**: Drag a window by tap-and-hold low for a moment, then move; lift to release.
- **Say**: “Tap and hold low to drag. Lift to drop.”

### 1:05–1:12 — Scroll + quit
- **Do**: Middle finger up, move hand up/down to scroll.
- **Say**: “Middle finger up to scroll.”
- **Do**: Press `q`.
- **Say**: “Press **q** to quit.”

---

## Recording tips (keeps the video clear)
- **Lighting**: bright, even light on your hand.
- **Background**: plain wall if possible.
- **Hand distance**: about 1–3 feet from the camera.
- **On-screen**: keep the HUD visible (top-left) for mode + speed.
